# Do Indian passport holders need a visa for Japan?

## The exemption list

- Japan has reciprocal visa-exemption arrangements with **74 countries and regions**, listed on MOFA's "Exemption of Visa (Short-Term Stay)" page, dated September 1, 2025.
- **India is not on that list.** Indian nationals therefore need a visa even for a short-term stay such as tourism. For tourism, people residing in India can apply for JAPAN eVISA through an accredited agency; see the eVISA document.
- For exempt nationalities the usual stay is 90 days, but 15 days for Indonesia and Thailand and 30 days for Brunei and Qatar. This doesn't apply to Indian passports.

## Practical notes

- If you hold another nationality (for example you are an Indian resident with a different passport), check the list for **your passport's** country.
- Rules change. Check the official page before you book.
