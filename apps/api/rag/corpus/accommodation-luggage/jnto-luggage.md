# Coin lockers, luggage storage and luggage delivery

## Coin lockers

- At most stations and tourist sites. JNTO lists **300 to 600 yen** depending on size.
- Many accept IC transit cards.
- Lockers at busy stations fill up quickly.

## Luggage delivery ("Hands-Free Travel")

- Look for counters with the **Hands-Free Travel** logo, drop your bags and fill in a delivery form.
- Options include same-day delivery, delivery on a set date and time, multilingual support, tracking, and compensation for loss or damage.
- Narita, Haneda and Kansai airports offer delivery to your hotel. Most major hotels can arrange hotel-to-hotel transfers at the front desk. Some smaller places use a nearby convenience store.
- Carriers JNTO lists: Yamato Transport and JAL ABC.

## Limits

- Up to **160 cm** total (length + width + height), about **25–30 kg**, and contents worth under **300,000 yen**.
- **Not accepted:** valuables, fragile items, fresh food, liquids, and dangerous goods.
- Keep your passport, tickets, cards and cash with you, never in forwarded luggage.

## Suggestion (ours, not from the JNTO page)

Sending big suitcases ahead (for example Tokyo to Kyoto) lets you ride the Shinkansen with a small bag. Ask about the cut-off time and delivery date when you send them.
