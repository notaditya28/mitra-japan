# Japan Visitor Hotline

## Number

- From inside Japan: **050-3816-2787**
- From overseas: **+81-50-3816-2787**

## When and in which languages

- Open 24 hours a day, 365 days a year.
- Languages: English, Chinese and Korean (the emergencies page also lists Japanese).

## What it helps with

- Help when you have an accident or fall ill.
- Help during natural disasters.
- General tourist information.

It is a support line, **not** an emergency dispatcher. For an ambulance or fire call 119; for the police call 110.
