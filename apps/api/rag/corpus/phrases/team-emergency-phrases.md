# Japanese phrases for emergencies

These phrases help you ask people nearby for help. They do **not** replace calling emergency services: **119** for an ambulance or fire, **110** for the police (see the FDMA and JNTO documents). The translations are drafts pending native-speaker review.

## Ambulance

- 救急車を呼んでください。
- Kyūkyūsha o yonde kudasai.
- "Please call an ambulance."

## Nearest hospital

- 一番近い病院はどこですか？
- Ichiban chikai byōin wa doko desu ka?
- "Where is the nearest hospital?"

## Lost

- 道に迷いました。この住所まで行く方法を教えてください。
- Michi ni mayoimashita. Kono jūsho made iku hōhō o oshiete kudasai.
- "I am lost. Please help me get to this address."
