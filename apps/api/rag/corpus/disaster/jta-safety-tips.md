# Safety Tips (Japan Tourism Agency)

## What it is

Safety Tips is the Japan Tourism Agency's safety information service for international tourists. It is a website plus a "Safety tips" app. JNTO recommends it for typhoons, earthquakes, tsunamis and volcanic eruptions.

## Notices on the website

- Earthquake early warnings.
- Tsunami warnings.
- Weather warnings and advisories.
- Volcanic warnings and forecasts.

## Languages

The website is in English, Korean, Traditional Chinese and Simplified Chinese.

## Suggestion for Indian travellers

Install the app before you leave India, and turn on notifications. This is our suggestion, not wording from the page.
