# Onsen (hot spring) and public bath etiquette

## Before getting in

1. Undress completely in the changing room. In most onsen, baths are taken without clothes.
2. **Wash thoroughly** at the shower stool before you enter the bath. The water is shared, so this is the most important rule.

## In the bath

- Keep your towel **out of the water**.
- Tie up long hair.
- Don't swim.

## Tattoos

- Many onsen **do not allow tattoos**. Some municipal baths, outdoor-style baths and local sento (public baths) are more tolerant.
- Check with the facility before you go.

## Other options

- Some mixed-gender baths require a swimsuit, which they provide.
- If you're uncomfortable sharing, private rental baths are an option. This can suit families or couples.
