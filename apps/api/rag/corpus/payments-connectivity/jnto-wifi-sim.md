# Staying connected: Wi-Fi, SIM cards and pocket Wi-Fi

## Free Wi-Fi

- **Airports:** Narita, Haneda, Kansai, New Chitose, Centrair (Nagoya) and Fukuoka.
- **Hotels:** most major city hotels have it. Traditional ryokan and minshuku, especially in mountain or rural areas, may not, so check when booking.
- **SoftBank Wi-Fi Passport (foreign phones):** two weeks of free access to hotspots nationwide. Set roaming to SoftBank and dial *8180 to get a password. The terms may have changed.
- Many cafes, fast-food chains and convenience stores offer free Wi-Fi after sign-up, but it can be slow.

## Pocket Wi-Fi

- JNTO calls it the safest option for coverage across Japan, including rural areas.
- Rental kiosks are at all major airports; you can also reserve online and have it delivered to your hotel.

## SIM cards

- Sold at major airports and at electronics stores such as Bic Camera and Yodobashi Camera.
- JNTO gives a rough guide of about 3,000 yen for 1 GB over 30 days. Treat this as an estimate and compare current plans.
- Your phone must be unlocked to use a local SIM.

## Suggestion (ours, not from the JNTO page)

Check with your Indian mobile operator whether international roaming works in Japan and what it costs.
