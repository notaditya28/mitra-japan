# Kiyomizu-dera Temple, Kyoto

## About

- A temple founded over 1,250 years ago, partway up Mt. Otowa in Kyoto's Higashiyama hills.
- Its main image is Kannon, the bodhisattva of mercy and compassion.

## Highlights on the grounds

- **Nio-mon:** the main entrance gate.
- **Hondo (Main Hall):** rebuilt in 1633 on the steep cliff, with the famous wooden stage.
- **Okuno-in Hall:** above the Otowa Waterfall, with a view back to the Main Hall stage and the city.
- **Otowa Waterfall:** three streams of water that visitors catch with ladles to pray for purification.
- **Jojuin:** its Moon Garden opens to the public only at limited times each year.

## Visiting

- **Opening hours and admission are not included here.** The temple publishes them on its official site, and special night visits and events change by season. Check before you go.
- The site has a wheelchair map, and Kyoto Wi-Fi is listed on the grounds page.
- It is a hillside site with slopes and steps. Our suggestion: allow extra time and rest stops for elderly travellers.
