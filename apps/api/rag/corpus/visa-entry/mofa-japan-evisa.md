# JAPAN eVISA for short-term tourism

## What it is

The Ministry of Foreign Affairs of Japan runs JAPAN eVISA, an online system for people who want to visit Japan for short-term tourism. Approved applicants receive an electronic visa instead of a visa sticker in the passport.

## Who can use it (as listed on the page dated May 15, 2026)

- People residing in India are listed among those who must apply **through an accredited agency** (the same group includes Hong Kong, Indonesia, Macau, Mongolia, the Republic of Korea, Singapore and the United Arab Emirates). Those exempt from short-term visas do not need it.
- Only ordinary passport holders can apply through the eVISA website.
- It is for travel to Japan by air, or by the listed international scheduled ferries from Busan or Shanghai.
- The embassy or consulate covering the applicant's place of residence may ask the applicant to come in for an interview.

## What it covers

- A single-entry short-term stay visa for **tourism**, for a period of up to 90 days.
- Other purposes (for example business or visiting relatives) and long-term stays are applied for at the Japanese embassy, consulate general or consular office covering the applicant's residence, sometimes through accredited travel agencies. Those visas are issued as a sticker in the passport.

## At the airport

Travellers with an eVISA must show the "Visa issuance notice" on screen with an internet connection. The page states that a PDF, screenshot or printed copy is not accepted.

## Warnings on the page

- Beware of fraudulent websites that look like the JAPAN eVISA site.
- Incomplete applications are cancelled without processing and must be resubmitted; wrong information or documents can delay or cancel the application.
