# Japan Rail Pass

## What it is

The six JR Group companies jointly offer the Japan Rail Pass. With it you can ride JR trains across the network as often as you like, including Shinkansen bullet trains and limited express trains. The site says the JR network is over 19,000 km, from Hokkaido to Kyushu.

## What it does not cover

**NOZOMI and MIZUHO** trains on the Tokaido and Sanyo Shinkansen need a separate special ticket. Pass holders can buy it. The site notes that up to 12 NOZOMI trains an hour run between Tokyo and Shin-Osaka.

## Buying and reserving seats

- Buy the pass on the official Japan Rail Pass website.
- You can reserve seats in advance only if you bought the pass on the official website.
- Seats can be reserved online or at reserved-seat ticket machines, which avoids queues at ticket offices.
