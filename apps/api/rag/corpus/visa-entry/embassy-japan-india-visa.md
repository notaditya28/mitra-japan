# Applying for a Japan visa from India

## Where to apply

- Applications from India are submitted **through VFS Global** (the embassy's page says no prior appointment is needed). VFS site: https://visa.vfsglobal.com/ind/en/jpn/
- The embassy itself accepts only diplomatic and official visas, plus cases needing special attention on humanitarian grounds.
- Applications are accepted from **3 months before the travel date**. The embassy asks applicants to plan well ahead: processing times can vary a lot, and the process cannot be expedited for an applicant's schedule.
- The JAPAN eVISA system started for India on 1 April 2024 (see the MOFA eVISA document).

## Fees (as listed on the page, "from 1 July 2026")

- For Indian nationals, a single or multiple visa: **500 INR**, plus the VFS service fee.
- The page says the transit visa has been abolished.
- Fees change; always check the current amount on the embassy page.

## Inquiries

- Japan Visa Information Hotline (listed on the page): +91-(0)11-7181-6614, open 24×7, English only.
- VFS call centre (listed on the page): +91-(0)22-6786-6016.
- The embassy does not answer questions about application status, documentation or reasons for refusal.

## Fraud warning

You only pay the visa fee and the service charge shown on the page. The embassy never asks for payment by phone or email to verify documents or speed things up. If someone contacts you like this, don't respond; the embassy suggests reporting it to the police and telling the embassy.

## Other notes linked from the page

- Visit Japan Web (online entry procedures).
- Do not bring meat products, vegetables or fruits into Japan (Ministry of Agriculture notice).
- Get overseas travel insurance before going to Japan.
- Relaxed requirements exist for Indian students (2017) and for multiple-entry visas for some Indian nationals (2019); see the embassy page for details.
