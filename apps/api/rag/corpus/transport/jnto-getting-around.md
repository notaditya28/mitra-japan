# Getting around Japan

## Trains

- **JR** (Japan Railways) runs the largest train network.
- **Shinkansen** bullet trains are the fastest way between major cities.
- **Local railways** are slower but show regional scenery.
- **Subways** cover city centres.
- Trains and buses run very close to their timetables.
- JNTO links to regional JR rail passes and other discount passes and travel cards.

## Buses, taxis and flights

- Local and highway buses run day and night to many destinations.
- Taxis are described as clean and reliable. **Carry cash**, because not every taxi accepts cards.
- Domestic flights reach every part of the country. This matters for Okinawa and Hokkaido.

## Other options

Bicycles, car rental, ferries and island travel, and luggage delivery (see the luggage document).
