# Todai-ji Temple, Nara

## About

Todai-ji is a temple in Nara with a history going back to the 8th century. Its best-known building is the **Daibutsu-den** (Great Buddha Hall). The site also includes a museum and the Shoso-in.

## Visiting

- The homepage lists the Daibutsu-den as open **7:30 to 17:30**. It doesn't say for which season, so treat this as unverified and check the official "Opening Hours / Admission Fees" page.
- Fees are on that separate official page; they are not listed here.
- **Smoking and drone photography are prohibited** in the temple precincts.
- Bicycles and motorcycles must be parked in the lot southwest of the Great South Gate. Pushing them into the precincts is not allowed.

## Where to check

https://www.todaiji.or.jp/en/ (Todai-ji office address on the site: 406-1 Zoushi-cho, Nara.)
