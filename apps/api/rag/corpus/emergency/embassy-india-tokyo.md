# Embassy of India, Tokyo

## Address and hours

- Embassy of India, 2-2-11 Kudan-Minami, Chiyoda-ku, Tokyo 102-0074.
- Nearest station: Kudanshita Station (Hanzomon, Shinjuku and Tozai subway lines), Exit 2.
- Open 9:00 am to 5:30 pm, Monday to Friday. Closed on Saturdays, Sundays and the embassy's public holidays.
- Consular section, Monday to Friday: applications accepted 09:30–11:30, passports and documents delivered 16:00–17:00.

## Helplines (as listed on the homepage)

- **Emergency helpline, only for Indian nationals in distress** (not for consular queries): **+81-80330-15242**, 24×7, by direct call, WhatsApp or SMS. Email: sscons.tokyo@mea.gov.in.
- **Passport, police, hospital, medical, birth, death and other consular matters**: +81-80710-94857, 09:00–17:30 JST on working days (call, WhatsApp or SMS). Email: cons.tokyo@mea.gov.in.
- General queries only: +81-90389-29291 (24×7).
- Reception: +81-3-3262-2397, 09:00–17:30 JST on working days.
- When you message by WhatsApp or SMS, include your name, address, contact number and the nature of your query.

## Which mission covers you (Tokyo's consular jurisdiction)

The Tokyo embassy covers:

- Hokkaido and Tohoku.
- Kanto (including Tokyo, Kanagawa, Chiba, Saitama).
- Yamanashi, Nagano, Niigata, Toyama, Ishikawa and Fukui.
- Gifu, Shizuoka and Aichi.
- Okinawa.

The embassy site also lists **Consulates of India in Osaka and Fukuoka**. Travellers in other regions, such as Kyoto, Osaka, Nara or Hiroshima, should check which consulate covers them.

## If your Indian passport is lost or destroyed

- Report it **immediately** to the nearest Indian embassy or consulate **and to the local police**.
- The consular wing handles all Indian passport services, including **Emergency Certificates**, and Identity Certificates.
- New machine-readable passports are processed centrally in New Delhi and take about **4 to 6 weeks**. Ask the mission about an Emergency Certificate if you need to travel before then.
- Before you travel, keep a photocopy of your passport separate from the passport itself. Include the personal details page, validity, date and place of issue, your departure stamp from India, your entry stamp for Japan and your Japanese visa.
