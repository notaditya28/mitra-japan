# Tax-free shopping, paying and bargaining

## Tax-free shopping for visitors

- Look for the "Tax-Free Shop" logo, or ask staff.
- Bring your **passport**.
- The minimum spend is **5,000 yen**, for general goods and for consumables.
- Take the items, receipt and passport to the tax-free counter. The tax is deducted there, and items go into a sealed tax-free bag.
- Keep your Record of Purchase and **show it to customs when you leave Japan**.
- Tax-free rules change. Check the official tax-free guide linked from the page before you rely on them.

## Cash or card

Cards are increasingly accepted, but Japan is still largely cash-based, especially in small shops. Carry enough cash for small purchases.

## Bargaining

Most shops don't negotiate. Large chains may offer price matching. Small private shops occasionally make a deal; asking is fine, but expect to pay the marked price.
