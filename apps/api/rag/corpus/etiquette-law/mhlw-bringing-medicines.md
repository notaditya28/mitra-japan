# Bringing medicines into Japan for personal use

## Without an Import Confirmation (Yakkan Shoumei)

You can bring these amounts for your own use without applying:

- **Prescription drugs, poisons and deadly poisons:** up to a **1-month supply**.
- **Other drugs and quasi-drugs:** up to a **2-month supply**.
- **External-use drugs:** up to **24 per item**.
- **Injectables and syringes:** up to a 1-month supply, only as pre-filled syringes or self-injection kits.
- **Cosmetics:** up to 24 per item.
- **Medical devices:** one household device, or up to a 2-month supply of disposable contact lenses.

## Larger amounts

You need an **Import Confirmation**. Apply and get approval **before leaving home**, then show it to customs. Some applications can be filed online through MHLW's Import Confirmation site.

## Narcotics and stimulant ingredients

- Importing narcotics and stimulant raw materials is **strictly prohibited**. The only exception is a patient carrying prescribed amounts who has **permission in advance** from a regional bureau of health and welfare.
- Prohibited narcotics and stimulant raw materials cannot be brought in under any circumstances.

## Our suggestion (not from the page)

Carry your prescription or a doctor's letter in English with your medicines. If you're unsure about a specific medicine, check with MHLW before travelling.
