# Missed trains, ticket changes, refunds and delays (JR East)

These are JR East's published rules. The page says it was "current as of March 2021". Tokaido Shinkansen trains (Tokyo–Kyoto–Osaka) are run by JR Central, so for those trains ask at the station.

## If you miss your train

- Limited express reserved-seat tickets and Green Car tickets are **not valid after the specified train leaves, and are not refunded**.
- With a limited express reserved-seat ticket, you can still ride **later the same day in a non-reserved seat in an Ordinary car**. If you sit in a reserved seat, you pay the full reserved-seat charge again.
- On these all-reserved JR East trains, a missed reserved ticket can be changed to a **standing place on a later train the same day**:
  - Hayabusa, Komachi, Hayate, Kagayaki
  - Hitachi, Tokiwa, Narita Express, Swallow Akagi
  - for Hayabusa and Komachi, only with a ticket for those trains
- On other all-reserved express trains, a missed ticket does **not** let you board a later all-reserved train.

## Changing a ticket before travel

- An unused ticket can be changed **once**, free of charge, to another ticket of the same category. You pay or get back any fare difference.
- For a second change, refund the ticket (a handling charge applies) and buy a new one.
- Reserved-seat tickets can be changed only **before the original train departs**, at a JR Ticket Office (Midori-no-madoguchi) while it is selling reserved seats.

## Refunds

- Unused, valid tickets can be refunded minus a cancellation charge.
- Issued seat tickets **cannot be refunded after the train's departure time**.
- Passes cannot be exchanged for another pass or a ticket, and their validity cannot be changed.

## Cancellations and delays

- If a trip is cancelled because the train service is cancelled, **fares and charges are fully refunded**. If service stops partway, the unused part of the fare and the express charges are refunded.
- If a limited express or express arrives **two hours or more late**, the express charges are fully refunded.
- Refund requests for cancellations and delays are accepted for up to one year. Ask at a JR Ticket Office or a travel agent.
- JR East is **not responsible** for extra hotel or transport costs caused by delays. Passes are not extended or refunded because of delays.

## Lost tickets

If you lose a ticket or it's stolen, ask at a ticket office or a train attendant to buy it again. The repurchased ticket is marked, and you keep it as a receipt.

## Where to check

https://www.jreast.co.jp/en/multi/ticket/changes.html. Station staff at a JR Ticket Office can confirm the rule for your exact ticket.
