# Lost Indian passport in Kansai or western Japan (Osaka-Kobe consulate)

## Who this applies to

This is the guidance from the Consulate General of India, Osaka-Kobe. It applies to Indian tourists who lose their passport in the consulate's area:

- **Kansai:** Mie, Shiga, **Kyoto**, **Osaka**, Hyogo, **Nara**, Wakayama.
- **Chugoku:** Tottori, Shimane, Okayama, **Hiroshima** (including Miyajima), Yamaguchi.
- **Shikoku:** Tokushima, Kagawa, Ehime, Kochi.

Koyasan is in Wakayama, so it is covered too.

## Steps

1. **Immediately** tell the nearest Lost & Found department, report the loss at the nearest **police station**, and get a **police report**.
2. **Inform the consulate:**
   - Tel: +81-6-6261-7299 or +81-6-6261-9299
   - Emergency numbers: **+81-80-2524-1798** or **+81-80-8805-2207**
3. **Apply online** for an Emergency Certificate at https://mportal.passportindia.gov.in/mission and print the application after you get an ARN number.
4. **Go to the consulate in person** with:
   - the Emergency Certificate application form
   - one passport-size photo, 3 cm × 3 cm, taken in the last six months
   - the fee of **JPY 2530**
   - the police report
   - a copy of the lost passport, or other proof of Indian nationality such as a driving licence or voter ID
   - a copy of your flight details or air tickets

## Important

- An Emergency Certificate is a **one-time travel document only for travelling back to India**.
- Generating the Emergency Certificate **automatically cancels** the lost passport.
