# Staying safe in Japan

## Useful phone numbers (as listed by JNTO)

- **110**: police emergency number.
- **03-3501-0110**: Tokyo Metropolitan Police English helpline, 24 hours a day, 365 days a year.
- **050-3816-2787**: JNTO Japan Visitor Hotline for emergency support such as illness or disasters. 24 hours, every day. English, Chinese, Korean and Japanese.
- **050-3416-0010**: JNTO TIC Call Center for general tourist information, 9 a.m. to 5 p.m. every day. This line is not for emergencies.
- **03-5774-0992**: Tokyo English Life Line (TELL) for mental health support, Monday to Thursday 9 a.m. to 11 p.m. and Friday to Sunday 9 a.m. to 2 a.m.

This page does not list the ambulance or fire number. See the separate emergency document for that.

## Petty crime and the koban

Crime is low in Japan, but petty crime can happen. For help, go to a **koban**, one of the small neighbourhood police boxes. If you don't know where one is, ask someone.

## Lost or stolen passport, cards or tickets

1. **Get a police report.** You need one to have a passport reissued or to make an insurance claim. A local police station or koban can make it. If you are in a hotel, ask the front desk to help.
2. **Contact your embassy** to arrange a replacement passport. Note your embassy's address and contact details before you travel.
3. **Contact the issuers** of lost credit cards or airline tickets to arrange replacements.
4. Before the trip, make copies of your passport: keep one with you and leave one at home. Keep a list of important numbers.

## Feeling ill

- The Japan Tourism Agency's "Guide for when you are feeling ill" explains how to find medical institutions, in English, Chinese and Korean.
- The AMDA International Medical Information Center offers a multilingual medical questionnaire.

## Insurance

JNTO advises buying travel insurance with adequate cover before you leave.
