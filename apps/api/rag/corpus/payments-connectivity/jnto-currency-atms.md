# Money, currency exchange and ATMs

## Yen

- Notes: ¥1,000, ¥2,000, ¥5,000 and ¥10,000.
- Coins listed on the page: ¥1, ¥5, ¥10, ¥50 and ¥500.
- Few ¥2,000 notes are in circulation and they can be mistaken for ¥1,000 notes, so check your change.

## Cash or card

- JNTO calls Japan **"very much a cash-based society"**. Carry some cash, especially for small shops, rural areas and taxis.
- Airports accept international cards for train tickets, taxis and many services.

## Exchanging money

- Exchange counters in airport arrival halls, banks, some hotels and currency shops.
- Many bank branches close at 3 p.m. Rates change daily.

## ATMs that take foreign cards

- **Japan Post Bank**: mostly in post offices, also at some stations and supermarkets. English available; hours vary.
- **Seven Bank**: mainly in **7-Eleven** stores, with guidance in 12 languages.
- **Mizuho**, **Mitsubishi UFJ** and **SMBC** branches accept some international cards (see the page for which).
- ATMs at Narita, Haneda, Chubu Centrair and Kansai airports.
- Fees can depend on the time and day. Not all ATMs are open 24 hours, and some central ATMs may run out of cash.
