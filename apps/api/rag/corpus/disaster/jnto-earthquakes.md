# Earthquakes, tsunami and disaster information

## Natural hazards in Japan

Japan is prone to typhoons, earthquakes, tsunamis and volcanic eruptions. Most events are small, but JNTO advises visitors to be prepared. Earthquakes can happen at any time with little or no warning.

## During a larger earthquake

1. **Stay calm** and follow any instructions from staff.
2. Put out open flames as quickly as possible. Fire is the most dangerous secondary disaster.
3. If you are **near the coast** when a large earthquake strikes, **go to higher ground** in case of a tsunami.

## Prepare in advance

- When you check into a hotel, find the evacuation routes.
- Keep your phone and other devices fully charged.

## Where to get disaster information

JNTO lists these sources for typhoons, earthquakes, tsunamis or volcanic eruptions:

- **Japan Safe Travel Information** (website)
- **Safety Tips** (app from the Japan Tourism Agency)
- **NHK WORLD-JAPAN** (website and app)
- The **JNTO Japan Visitor Hotline, 050-3816-2787**, gives emergency support including during disasters, 24 hours a day.
