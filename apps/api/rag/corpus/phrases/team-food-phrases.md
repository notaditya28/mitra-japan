# Japanese phrases for food and dietary needs

Show these to staff, or read the romaji aloud. They are the same phrases as the in-app staff cards. The translations are drafts pending native-speaker review.

## Vegetarian

- 私はベジタリアンです。肉、魚、卵は食べません。
- Watashi wa bejitarian desu. Niku, sakana, tamago wa tabemasen.
- "I am vegetarian. I do not eat meat, fish or eggs."

## No fish stock

- 魚のだし（かつお節など）を使わないでください。
- Sakana no dashi (katsuobushi nado) o tsukawanaide kudasai.
- "Please do not use fish stock (like bonito flakes)."

## Jain

- ジャイナ教の食事制限があります。肉・魚・卵・玉ねぎ・にんにく・根菜は食べられません。
- Jaina-kyō no shokuji seigen ga arimasu. Niku, sakana, tamago, tamanegi, ninniku, konsai wa taberaremasen.
- "I follow a Jain diet: no meat, fish, egg, onion, garlic or root vegetables."

## Halal

- 豚肉、ラード、お酒（みりんを含む）は食べられません。
- Butaniku, rādo, osake (mirin o fukumu) wa taberaremasen.
- "I cannot eat pork, lard or alcohol (including mirin)."

## No beef

- 牛肉は食べられません。
- Gyūniku wa taberaremasen.
- "I do not eat beef."

## No egg

- 卵を抜いてください。
- Tamago o nuite kudasai.
- "No egg, please."

## Asking about ingredients

- これに肉、魚、または魚のだしは入っていますか？
- Kore ni niku, sakana, mata wa sakana no dashi wa haitte imasu ka?
- "Does this contain meat, fish or fish stock?"
